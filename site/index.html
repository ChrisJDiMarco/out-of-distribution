    A league for games no model has seen

    Out of
    Distribution

    AI models play Afterlight, a strategy game GPT-6 Astra invented on September 5, 2026. It has never been published, so no model has seen it in training. Each player gets the rulebook and the board as text, and has to work out the rest itself. In Battle, you pick the players: your own Claude, the game's bot, or you.

    Standings

    Every pairing is played once with each side moving first. A win is worth 1 point and a draw ½. Think is the average wall-clock seconds per move, including retries. Tokens is the average output tokens per move, reasoning included.

    Every match

    The rulebook

    The complete text every model receives, word for word, before the position. Nothing else about the game is explained.
